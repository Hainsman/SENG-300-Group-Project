## Team Responsibilities ##

### 1. Client/UI + Testing Team
**Primary Focus:** User-facing experience, usability, and quality assurance

**Responsibilities:**
- Design and implement the client-side application (UI/UX)
- Build user interfaces that enable seamless interaction with the multiplayer game platform
- Ensure accessibility, visual clarity, and design consistency across all screens.
- Translate game and platform requirements into intuitive and user-friendly workflows
- Develop and execute testing plans (unit, integration, and system testing)
- Identify, document, and track bugs and usability issues
- Verify that completed features meet functional and non-functional requirements
- Collaborate closely with the Platform Core and Rules + Validation teams to ensure end-to-end functionality is properly tested.

---

### 2. Rules + Validation Team
**Primary Focus:** Game logic, rules, and correctness

**Responsibilities:**
- Design and implement the rules for all supported games
- Define game states, transitions, and win/loss conditions
- Enforce rule validation and prevent illegal game actions
- Ensure the consistency and correctness of all gameplay logic.
- Develop validation logic that integrates smoothly with the platform backend
- Provide clear documentation of game rules and edge cases
- Work with Client/UI to ensure rules are reflected accurately in the interface
- Support the Integration team during system-level testing and validation

---

### 3. Platform Core Team
**Primary Focus:** Backend systems, networking, and core infrastructure

**Responsibilities:**
- Design and implement the core platform architecture
- Manage networking, multiplayer communication, and state synchronization
- Manage game sessions, players, and server-side state
- Implement APIs and services used by the client and game logic
- Ensure performance, scalability, and reliability of the platform
- Manage data storage, messaging systems, and error handling mechanisms
- Provide clear interfaces for integration with other teams
- Support debugging and performance optimization efforts

---

### 4. Integration, Release, and Project Health Team
**Primary Focus:** System cohesion, delivery, and process management

**Responsibilities:**
- Integrate components from all teams into a single cohesive system
- Provide cross-team support until system components are ready for integration
- Manage builds, releases, and versioning
- Coordinate merges, resolve integration conflicts, and maintain stability
- Define and enforce development workflows and standards
- Track project progress, risks, and blockers
- Maintain project documentation and deliverable status
- Support sprint planning, retrospectives, and reporting
- Serve as the bridge between technical development and project management requirements

## Member Responsibilities ##

Communication:
- Ask questions when needed and maintain professional and respectful communication with team members
- Respond to messages on Discord within 24h
- Respond to Discord polls when a team decision is needed
- Participate in Scrum Daily standup 10am-12pm (Monday/Wednesday/Friday)
- Be proactive and provide solutions/feedback in decisions that involve the team

Work:
- Develop code in branches
- Commit 3 hours of work weekly outside of tutorial and lecture hours
- Commit to git regularly and provide informative comments/notes
- Maintain an up-to-date work log documenting your contributions
- Be prepared to clearly explain your contributions during presentations

## Sub-Team Leader Responsibilities ##

- Make sure the subteam's designs stay on course with the group's vision of the project
- Keep the team focused on the "big picture" as opposed to specific features of the design
- Reach out to other subteams whenever necessary in order to maintain cohesion across the project
- Delegate tasks to subteam members fairly and effectively
- Be prepared to take the lead in demos and presentations
- Monitor and maintain the subteam’s issue board to ensure it remains up to date
- Support subteam members in resolving conflicts and removing roadblocks
